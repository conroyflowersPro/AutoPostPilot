## Success (question 6)

Success is **not** “drafts exist” and **not** “text-only auto-publish right after generate.”

Success is this loop:

1. Operator presses **7-day generate** (`/generate`).
2. ORDER 0–8 (Grok 4.6) infers seeds and writes posts **inside the rules**, as `@Seung4680`, not as visible AI. New week must not collide with the previous batch or with posts from the last **30 days**, except labeled experiments (below).
3. Operator **reviews** those posts and attaches **original** images or videos.
4. **After** review + media, AI **automates publishing** (Fedica). At that moment AI infers and **decides** the gaps between posts from X-algorithm evidence. It does not ask the operator to pick times.
5. Published outcomes feed learning. Handmade posts and AP-posted posts stay **separate populations**.
6. Over weeks, the **X account grows** via reader participation.

Human gate = review + original media. Machine gate = spacing + Fedica send. Neither gate may be skipped.

Pain point (question 5) is deferred: it will be named while we work.

## What AutoPostPilot is (operator intent)

Two jobs only.

### 1. See the account

The operator must easily see **this X account’s posts and comment/reply status**. That is a dashboard job. It is not AI writing replies.

- In: calendar, post list, scheduled/published state, comment/reply counts and threads as **status**
- Out of AI surface (still frozen): reply 제안, reply 다듬기, AI가 대신 댓글을 씀

### 2. Act on the account

AI writes as the user. After the operator reviews and attaches original media, AI **publishes**.

Conditions:

- Topic and prose must not read as “an AI wrote this”
- Write as `@Seung4680`’s account, not as a generic model
- **No publish until review + original media.** Text-only Fedica fire after generate is forbidden
- **After that gate, publish is automated.** AI infers and decides post **intervals** from X-algorithm evidence; the operator does not pick the clock
- Learn participation mechanisms from what happens on X, keeping **handmade vs AP-posted** distinct
- Next 7-day plan must not collide/repeat the last week or the last **month**, except 2–3 experimental repeats (below)
- Goal is X-account growth
- X policy always wins

“Viral” here means *how a reader enters and reacts* (ORDER 2). It does **not** mean bait, spam, or blasting text without media.

## Situation 4 — Performance DNA (proposal)

Role: help **posts grow** by learning from published outcomes. The account is **still in buildup**. Small numbers are expected. Do not wait for “enough likes/views” before learning.

**Buildup-phase participation (this is the learning trigger):**

| Signal | Counts as participation? | How to use now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reply (댓글) ≥ 1** | Yes. One comment **is** reader participation | Learn *that a person entered*. Strongest current how-signal |
| **Repost (재게시) ≥ 1** | Yes | Someone thought it worth passing on |
| **Bookmark ≥ 1** | Yes | Utility / want-to-return |
| Quote ≥ 1 | Yes | Discussion / judgment |
| Likes, impressions, views | Record only | Too noisy at this scale to teach *how* to write |
| Followers / profile visits | Lagging | Outcome of repeated participation, not a per-post recipe |
| Revenue | Only with real evidence | Do not invent |

Presence beats magnitude while the account is growing. A post with **1 reply and 12 likes** is a participation event. A post with **400 likes and 0 replies / 0 reposts / 0 bookmarks** is **not** a how-to-write lesson yet.

One participating post → `OBSERVATION` / `CANDIDATE`. Never auto-`VALIDATED` from a single week. Still: **do not ignore it** because the count is “only 1.”

Current `METRIC_WEIGHTS` (followers 40, revenue 15, replies 7) do not match this. When learning code is touched: rank **reply / repost / bookmark / quote presence first**; likes and impressions last; followers as lagging conversion.

Do not clone a participating seed subject. Transfer mechanism, entry, and wording quality only. A 1-reply post may justify a labeled experiment (2–3/week cap), not a copied week.

## 30-day anti-collision + experimental repeats

When a new 7-day batch is generated, it must be checked against:

1. The previous 7-day AP batch (even if not all are published yet)
2. Published posts (handmade **and** AP) from the last **~30 days**

Collision = same concrete subject, same angle, or high conceptual/wording overlap.

**Default:** reject / replace colliding seeds.

**Exception (tight):** 2–3 posts in that 7-day plan **may** repeat a recent subject **only if** all of these hold:

- There is a stated reason (not “we need volume”)
- The post is labeled an **experiment**
- The reason is “this participated (even 1 reply / repost / bookmark) and we are re-testing a *specific* learned part” (entry, mechanism, media, wording — **not** the whole post cloned)
- Cap: **2–3 per 7 days**, never a repeated week

Without that hypothesis, repetition is a failed week, not a diversity miss.

Current code window is often **21 days** of `account_activities` and leakage is **manual-only**. Direction is **30 days** and **handmade + AP**, plus the experiment cap.

## X policy (always wins)

X platform rules are **unconditional**. They override volume targets, growth tactics, and “fill the week.”

If a post would violate X rules, it must **not** become a publishable draft. Fail closed. A short week that stays on-policy is a valid week. A full week that would spam, duplicate, mislead, or manipulate the platform is a failed week.

Product-level X-policy constraints (not a complete legal engine; the live [X Rules](https://help.x.com/rules-and-policies) still govern):

- No spam, duplicate, or near-duplicate posting
- No scraped / paraphrase-farm content
- No fake engagement, bait-and-switch, or platform manipulation
- No invented experience, impersonation, or misleading claims
- Automation after **review + original media** is Fedica publish. Text-only fire right after generate is forbidden.
- Spacing and daily caps must not look like a spam firehose — X-algorithm gaps are also a policy control
- Growth tactics that break X rules are out of scope, even if they would add followers

Authenticity, originality, and anti-dumping already in the engine are in service of this rule. They are not optional style preferences.

## What v11 does

Four stages. Nothing else is the product.

| Stage | Job | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SEED** | Infer concrete subjects inside the interest area; no 30-day collision except experiments | Grok 4.6, under rules |
| **WRITE** | Turn a seed into a post `@Seung4680` would actually publish | Grok 4.6, under rules |
| **SPACING** | Infer and **decide** gaps between posts from X-algorithm evidence | AI, only at publish time |
| **PUBLISH** | Send reviewed posts **with** original media | AI via Fedica, **after** operator review + media |
| **LEARN** | Published outcomes, split handmade vs AP | Performance DNA / Creator DNA |

### Seed

Sources (both required as inputs, not templates to clone):

1. `@Seung4680` handmade posts (Creator DNA / archive / recent originals)
2. X seeds **inside the user’s interest area** (Tesla/FSD/product observation, daily, gaming, LAFC, and current explicit intent)

Grok 4.6 infers the next concrete subject. Inference has rules. The engine must not emit registry templates, viral clones, invented firsthand experience, or a colliding subject from the last 30 days (except labeled experiments).

A seed is a direction, not a finished post.

### Write

Write inside the established rules (Creator DNA, claim/authenticity gates, editorial mode, everyday language, no invented experience).

The post must still pass a human test **and** X policy:

- Not mechanical
- Not a formula with different nouns
- Something `@Seung4680` would send as his own
- Something X would accept as authentic, non-spam original posting

### Spacing + publish (after review + media)

Two gates:

1. **Human:** review the week’s drafts and attach original image/video. Without this, nothing is sent.
2. **AI:** once a post (or the reviewed batch) has media and is confirmed, AI **automates Fedica publish** and **decides** the interval from X-algorithm evidence. The operator does not pick each clock time.

Hardcoded `17:00` LA + 3h + 5/day remains **fallback** while learned windows are weak. Fallback must still be policy-safe (anti-dump).

**Forbidden:** Fedica/X send of text-only drafts right after 7-day generate.

A post that fails authenticity, originality, 30-day collision (unless experiment), X-policy, or has no original media stays a blocked draft. It must not be silently dropped from the ledger.

### Learn

Only **published** posts enter DNA updates. Drafts are not success.

- Handmade published → Creator DNA / voice / intent (slow)
- AP published → Performance DNA / participation mechanisms (fast, candidates until repeated evidence)
- **Buildup:** 1 reply or 1 repost or 1 bookmark is a participation event; likes/views do not teach how
- Chat screenshots may add operator-approved notes **only with explicit consent**
- Never average handmade and AP into one template
- Never clone a winning subject; experiments must name which *part* is being re-tested
