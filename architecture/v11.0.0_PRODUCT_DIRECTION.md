## Success (question 6)

Success is **not** “drafts exist in the DB.”

Success is this loop:

1. Operator presses **7-day generate** (`/generate`).
2. Grok 4.6 infers seeds and writes posts **inside the rules**.
3. Posts that pass rules **and X policy** are **automatically scheduled** through Fedica Publishing API (dates/gaps from X-algorithm evidence).
4. They publish on X. Outcomes feed learning.
5. Over weeks, the **X account grows**.

The generate click is the user-initiated action. Per-post `reviewed` is not the success path. Calendar remains the place to see, stop, or recover a failed schedule — not a required approval step for a healthy week.

Pain point (question 5) is deferred: it will be named while we work.

## X policy (always wins)

X platform rules are **unconditional**. They override volume targets, growth tactics, and “fill the week.”

If a post would violate X rules, it must **not** be scheduled. Fail closed. A short week that stays on-policy is a valid week. A full week that would spam, duplicate, mislead, or manipulate the platform is a failed week.

Product-level X-policy constraints (not a complete legal engine; the live [X Rules](https://help.x.com/rules-and-policies) still govern):

- No spam, duplicate, or near-duplicate posting
- No scraped / paraphrase-farm content
- No fake engagement, bait-and-switch, or platform manipulation
- No invented experience, impersonation, or misleading claims
- Automation is allowed only as this operator’s explicit 7-day generate (not an unattended bot)
- Spacing and daily caps must not look like a spam firehose — X-algorithm gaps are also a policy control
- Growth tactics that break X rules are out of scope, even if they would add followers

Authenticity, originality, and anti-dumping already in the engine are in service of this rule. They are not optional style preferences.

## Who it is for (and whether “later, other creators” changes design)

**Now:** personal tool. One operator, one X account, one voice.

**Later:** other creators are possible, but that possibility must **not** reshape v11.

It would hurt the product if we generalized now:

- Seed / post prompts written for “any creator”
- Multi-tenant auth, onboarding, generic DNA schemas
- Softened authenticity so the engine is “reusable”

It does **not** require extra architecture if we keep one existing seam:

- Runtime identity stays `@Seung4680`
- Creator DNA + interest areas + writing rules stay **data**, not a SaaS product
- When/if another creator appears, the extension point is a new DNA profile — not a rewrite of the weekly engine

v11 rule: **optimize for this one writer.** Do not add abstraction “for later.”

## What v11 does

Four stages. Nothing else is the product.

| Stage | Job | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SEED** | Infer concrete subjects inside the creator’s interest area | Grok 4.6, under rules |
| **WRITE** | Turn a seed into a post `@Seung4680` would actually publish | Grok 4.6, under rules |
| **SCHEDULE** | Pick date and spacing from X-algorithm evidence; hand off to Fedica | System + Fedica Publishing API |
| **LEARN** | Published X outcomes update the next week’s seeds and timing | Performance DNA / 14d learning |

### Seed

Sources (both required as inputs, not templates to clone):

1. `@Seung4680` handmade posts (Creator DNA / archive / recent originals)
2. X seeds **inside the user’s interest area** (Tesla/FSD/product observation, daily, gaming, LAFC, and current explicit intent)

Grok 4.6 infers the next concrete subject. Inference has rules. The engine must not emit registry templates, viral clones, or invented firsthand experience as seed bodies.

A seed is a direction, not a finished post.

### Write

Write inside the established rules (Creator DNA, claim/authenticity gates, editorial mode, everyday language, no invented experience).

The post must still pass a human test **and** X policy:

- Not mechanical
- Not a formula with different nouns
- Something `@Seung4680` would send as his own
- Something X would accept as authentic, non-spam original posting

### Schedule

Publishing provider is **Fedica Publishing API** only.

After a successful 7-day generate, qualifying posts are scheduled automatically. Date and interval come from X-algorithm evidence for this account, **bounded by X policy** (anti-spam spacing, no dump).

Hardcoded `17:00` LA + 3h gap + 5/day is **fallback only** while learned windows are weak. Fallback must still be policy-safe.

A post that fails authenticity, originality, or X-policy checks stays unscheduled (`blocked` / not sent to Fedica). It must not be silently dropped from the ledger.

### Learn

Only **published** posts enter Performance DNA. Drafts and AI text are not success evidence. Learning may advise seed *quality* (entry, wording, media usefulness) and timing windows. It must never clone a winning seed subject.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **X policy always wins.** Growth never outranks platform rules.
2. Growth OS, not a viral optimizer. The outcome metric is X-account growth from on-policy posts.
3. Never invent firsthand experience or current facts without evidence.
4. Strategy is a hypothesis until published X outcomes validate it.
5. REPOST is manual only. REPOST text is not writing voice.
6. The 7-day generate click is the user-initiated automation. No unattended posting loop.
7. Publishing failures must not write into Planner Memory / DNA.
8. Inference model for seed and post generation is **Grok 4.6**.
