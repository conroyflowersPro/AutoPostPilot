## Success (question 6)

Success is **not** “drafts exist” and **not** “text-only auto-publish right after generate.”

Success is this loop:

1. Operator presses **7-day generate** (`/generate`).
2. ORDER 0–8 (Grok 4.6) infers seeds and writes posts **inside the rules**, as `@Seung4680`, not as visible AI. New week must not collide with the previous batch or with posts from the last **30 days**, except labeled experiments (below).
3. Operator **reviews** those posts and attaches **original** images or videos.
4. **After** review + media, AI **automates publishing** (Fedica). At that moment AI infers and **decides** the gaps between posts from X-algorithm evidence. It does not ask the operator to pick times.
5. Published outcomes feed learning. Handmade posts and AP-posted posts stay **separate populations**.
6. Over weeks, the **X account grows**. Strategy references the X algorithm. A question closer is fine when it matches how he writes; it is not installed to chase participation.

Human gate = review + original media. Machine gate = spacing + Fedica send. Neither gate may be skipped.

Pain point (question 5) is deferred: it will be named while we work.

## Core philosophy

AP infers. It does not paste examples. It assists posting and scheduling.

1. **No examples.** No finished-post samples, no “write like this,” no frozen mode→문체 maps.
2. **Data-based inference only.** Allowed data is exclusively:
   - what **X** can return (API / Analytics the operator’s account can access)
   - what **xAI API** can return
   - what the **operator provided** (file, chat screenshot with consent, explicit intent)
   Nothing else is evidence. Do not scrape unnamed sources or invent metrics.
3. **When establishing strategy, reference the X algorithm.** Spacing, mix, distribution, whether participation linked to **account growth**. Look at it for strategy. Do not let it write the post. Participation is not the product goal by itself.
4. **Question endings are allowed when they are his.** If USER_DIRECT posts (his experience, the way he actually wrote) end as a question, a comparable slot may end as a question. Forbidden is only the algorithm-driven formula: install a question / “어떻게 생각하세요?” / engagement closer *because* “X rewards participation.” That reads as AI. His own form does not.
5. **Do not invent experience or opinions.** Lived episodes stay on `as_of`. Stances must be grounded in what he has actually posted or explicitly given.
6. **Two tests together.** A draft is usable when (a) he would post it, and (b) data supports the pairing for participation **and**, when evidence exists, for growth. Operator review remains the gate.
7. **Distinguish who published, not who typed.** Most USER_DIRECT posts were AI-drafted; he still posted them himself. That population is not AP_PIPELINE.

### Algorithm vs last sentence

**Strategy** references the X algorithm: when to send, how far apart, mix of post types, whether a participation pattern actually moved **followers / reach / growth**.

The algorithm does **not** choose the last sentence.

**Allowed:** the post ends with a question because **he writes that way** — USER_DIRECT evidence (experience + posts he published) for that kind of slot.

**Forbidden:** add a question / “어떻게 생각하세요 / 댓글로 / 동의하시나요” / any engagement closer *because* the algorithm likes participation. That is the AI tell. ORDER 8 fails algorithm-driven closers, not his own question form.

## Situation 2 — Creator DNA: identity vs 말투 (proposal)

말투는 시시각각 변한다. **Archive must not teach how he sounds this week.**

Split Creator DNA into two clocks:

| Layer | What | Source | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Identity** | Who, interests, NOT THIS, experience ledger, authenticity | Archive episodes + stance (Tesla owner, no daytrade primary, never invent visits) | Slow |
| **말투 / register** | Endings (해요/음슴), length, ㅋㅋ, 존칭 vs 반말 mix, how a sentence actually lands | **Rolling handmade ORIGINALS only** | Fast |

**말투 window (proposal):**

1. Primary: last **14–30 days USER_DIRECT** ORIGINAL (he posted it; draft may be AI). Not REPLY, not REPOST, not AP_PIPELINE.
2. If fewer than ~5 in 30 days, extend to 60 days — still USER_DIRECT only. **Do not fall back to archive 6950.**
3. Rolling stats. One post does not flip the week.
4. Sync may refresh these stats in the engine.
5. **AP_PIPELINE never trains 말투.**
6. Reply DNA never averages into publishing 말투.

`generate-post/creator-style-data.ts` archive corpus (ending_style, haeyo/eumseum %, 6950 posts) is **legacy voice**. Stop using it as the writer’s SOT. Keep it off the 7-day write path.

CSV `/learning` hints do not override this rolling window.

Identity (WHO / NOT THIS / dated experience) may still sit in the compact planner block. That is not 말투.

### 말투 is inferred per slot (data, not a map)

7-day generate varies topic **and** 문체 because slots differ — not because a table assigned ㅋㅋ to one mode and banned it in another.

For each slot the writer infers 문체 from:

- Rolling handmade ORIGINALS in similar **character** (what he actually did in comparable posts)
- Mechanism / editorial intent as **context**, not as a lookup key to a canned 문체
- Participation and algorithm outcomes on those comparable handmade (and AP, as performance only) posts

If data is thin, infer conservatively toward what he would still post — still **without** an example paragraph and **without** a hardcoded ban list.

Judge asks: inferred 문체 + “would he send this” + no invented experience/opinion + no algorithm-installed closer. A question ending that matches USER_DIRECT form is allowed. It does **not** ask “does this match a cookbook.”

AP posts do not train the 말투 palette. Replies do not train publishing 문체.

## Situation 4 — Performance DNA (proposal)

Role: help **posts grow** by learning from published outcomes. The account is **still in buildup**. Small numbers are expected. Do not wait for “enough likes/views” before learning.

**Buildup-phase signals (two layers, both learned):**

| Signal | Layer | How to use now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reply ≥ 1** | Reader entry (how) | A person entered. Strongest writing/mechanism signal |
| **Repost ≥ 1** | Reader entry | Someone passed it on |
| **Bookmark ≥ 1** | Reader entry | Utility / want-to-return |
| **Quote ≥ 1** | Reader entry | Discussion / judgment |
| **Likes** | **X algorithm / distribution** | Ranking signal. Learn it. Do **not** treat as a sentence recipe |
| Impressions / views | X algorithm / reach | Pair with likes for “did this travel?” Log + spacing. Not voice |
| Followers / profile visits | Lagging conversion | After repeated entry + distribution |
| Revenue | Only with real evidence | Do not invent |

Likes are **not** ignored. X uses them. AP must learn like-velocity and like-presence for **spacing and distribution hypotheses**.

They still do **not** teach *how to write*. Do not translate “algorithm likes participation” into a sentence recipe. A question closer is valid only from USER_DIRECT form, never from this layer. A post with **400 likes and 0 replies / 0 reposts / 0 bookmarks** is an algorithm note (timing, media, cluster), not a voice lesson. A post with **1 reply and 12 likes** teaches entry *and* records modest distribution.

One participating post → `OBSERVATION` / `CANDIDATE`. Never auto-`VALIDATED` from a single week. “Only 1 reply” is still entry. Like ticks (12→15) **do** get a snapshot and may update the algorithm layer; they do **not** rewrite Creator DNA or clone a subject.

When learning code is touched: **entry presence first** (reply / repost / bookmark / quote); **likes + impressions as algorithm layer**; followers lagging. Do not drop likes to “unused.”

### Sync of existing posts (how to apply updates)

4. Stamp each snapshot with post age (EARLY / DAY / MULTI_DAY / MATURE).

Archive is **his lived record**. It is not permission to write that he went somewhere he never went, or that a past version happened *yesterday*.

Already sketched in ORDER experience code (`HISTORICAL` vs `TIMELESS`, `THEN_VS_NOW_SAFE`, `historical_framing_required`). That intent is right. Gaps: archive episode list is empty in the live edge (`ARCHIVE_EXPERIENCE_FALLBACK = []`); `temporalSafety` can allow “어제” when any `creator_evidence_available` is true — that is the “어제 FSD v10” hole.

### Forbidden

- Invent a visit / drive / city / test that is not in the ledger
- Move an old episode to now: archive has FSD v10 → 2026 post starts “어제 FSD v10을 경험했는데…”
- Treat an old field post as this week’s news: Robotaxi expands, so re-post the old service-area visit as if he went again

### Allowed (THEN vs NOW)

Current public fact + **framed** past episode:

> 예전에는 오스틴에서 이 정도가 서비스 지역이었는데, 오늘은 달라스까지 확장하네?

Rules for that shape:

- “Today / Dallas expansion” = current fact (X/news), not a claim he was in Dallas unless the ledger says so
- “예전 오스틴” = archive episode with `as_of`, written as past
- First person “가서 / 어제 / 오늘 타봤다” only if a **30-day handmade** (or ledger episode dated that day) backs it

### Episode ledger (what archive should be)

Each row: `what`, `where?`, `product_version?`, `as_of`, `last_verified`, `claim_class` (`LIVED` | `OBSERVED` | `NOT_LIVED`).

## 30-day anti-collision + experimental repeats

When a new 7-day batch is generated, it must be checked against:

1. The previous 7-day AP batch (even if not all are published yet)
2. Published posts (handmade **and** AP) from the last **~30 days**

Collision = same concrete subject, same angle, or high conceptual/wording overlap.

**Default:** reject / replace colliding seeds.

**Exception (tight):** 2–3 posts in that 7-day plan **may** repeat a recent subject **only if** all of these hold:

- There is a stated reason (not “we need volume”)
- The post is labeled an **experiment**
- The reason is “this participated (even 1 reply / repost / bookmark) and we are re-testing a *specific* learned part” (entry, mechanism, media, wording — **not** the whole post cloned)
- Cap: **2–3 per 7 days**, never a repeated week

Without that hypothesis, repetition is a failed week, not a diversity miss.

Current code window is often **21 days** of `account_activities` and leakage is **manual-only**. Direction is **30 days** and **handmade + AP**, plus the experiment cap.

## X policy (always wins)

X platform rules are **unconditional**. They override volume targets, growth tactics, and “fill the week.”

If a post would violate X rules, it must **not** become a publishable draft. Fail closed. A short week that stays on-policy is a valid week. A full week that would spam, duplicate, mislead, or manipulate the platform is a failed week.

Product-level X-policy constraints (not a complete legal engine; the live [X Rules](https://help.x.com/rules-and-policies) still govern):

- No spam, duplicate, or near-duplicate posting
- No scraped / paraphrase-farm content
- No fake engagement, bait-and-switch, or platform manipulation
- No invented experience, impersonation, or misleading claims
- Automation after **review + original media** is Fedica publish. Text-only fire right after generate is forbidden.
- Spacing and daily caps must not look like a spam firehose — X-algorithm gaps are also a policy control
- Growth tactics that break X rules are out of scope, even if they would add followers

Authenticity, originality, and anti-dumping already in the engine are in service of this rule. They are not optional style preferences.

**Archive “재추출” means:** running the 25GB X archive dump through the extractor (and/or sending that corpus to Grok) **again every time you press 7-day generate**. That is waste: slow, expensive, and 말투 must not come from archive anyway.

Correct path: extract **once** (or when you hand over a new dump) → small `experience-ledger.json` (dated lived episodes only) → weekly generate **reads that file**. Empty file = EXPERIENCE seeds stay closed. No ZIP on generate.

Naive “one xAI call per ORDER per post” would be ~250+ calls/week. **Not valid.** ORDER 1–6 were already local.

### Applied in v11 code

- `/generate` writes via weekly-plan `phase=write` (ORDER 7B), not `generate-post`
- Writer prompt is constraint-only, `max_tokens: 420`, no 6950-post corpus
- Expand stays **one** call; model `grok-4.6`
- Write concurrency 3; client chunks of 3 (timeout-safe)
- Reply suggest/polish return 410 without calling xAI
- Grok image route stays; generate does not call it
- Experience ledger is a JSON file; empty until the archive drop. Generate never re-reads the 25GB ZIP.

### Not worth doing

- Batch 6 posts in one writer call: cheaper, but breaks per-post isolation and reads as AI
- Downgrade writer to a tiny/fast model: saves money, hurts “would he post this”
- Skip expand xAI entirely: seeds collapse to thin local bootstrap

Rough order of magnitude: **~43 Grok 4.6 calls/week** (1 expand + 42 writes) with small prompts. Previous path was fewer calls but **much larger** prompts (style corpus every batch). Independent writer is the quality path; dropping the corpus is the cost path.
